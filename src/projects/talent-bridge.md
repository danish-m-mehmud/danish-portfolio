## Context

CMU Nerds’ internal staffing teams used Talent Bridge to source talent, match candidates to client engagements, and onboard them.

## What I delivered

I owned the Demand Request module from requirements through production support. I led the move of three staffing workflows to AGIOS using event-driven webhooks and PostgreSQL schema migrations with Prisma.

## Production outcome

Matching, Fulfillment, and Ramp-up moved into AGIOS, carrying the staffing workflows into the new platform.

## Context

High-volume eCommerce brands rely on Atomic to receive stock, track inventory, fulfill orders, process returns, and bill clients. Each warehouse rollout needed to account for how its staff handled locations, scanning, and stock rotation.

## What I delivered

I worked with clients and warehouse operators from requirements and data migration through configuration and post-launch tuning. I built receiving, inventory, fulfillment, returns, billing, and invoicing workflows, including barcode scanning, lot and expiry tracking, and FIFO and FEFO stock rotation. I integrated commerce platforms, carriers, and ERP systems so orders and inventory could stay in sync.

## Production outcome

A backend redesign tripled peak order processing capacity. I also built LangChain and LangGraph workflows for replenishment, receiving, and returns, plus a retrieval-augmented chatbot for 3PL support tickets that previously required manual handling.

I received a client appreciation award and performance bonus for my contributions to Atomic in April 2025.
